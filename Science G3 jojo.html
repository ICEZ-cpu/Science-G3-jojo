1. Different States of Weather

Chapter 5
  • Rain (Liquid): Made of water droplets that can flow.
  • Hailstones (Solid): Balls of ice that fall from the sky with a fixed shape.
  • Strong Wind (Gas): Fast-moving air around us.

3. Marbles vs Water in Containers

Chapter 5
  • Marbles (Solid): Cannot fill a beaker without gaps because marbles have a fixed shape.
  • Water (Liquid): Fills the beaker without gaps because water does not have a fixed shape and takes the shape of its container.

4. Mixtures, Solute & Solvent

Chapter 6
  • Mixture: Two or more materials mixed together (e.g., rice & salt, nuts & raisins). Materials retain their properties.
  • Soluble vs Insoluble: Salt & brown sugar dissolve in water (soluble). Sand & black pepper do not dissolve (insoluble).
  • Conservation of Mass: 330 g water + 10 g sugar = 340 g sugar water!

5. Separating Mixtures & Water Filters

Chapter 6
  • Sieve: Separates solids of different sizes (e.g., salt & rice; 1 cm, 2 cm, 3 cm beads).
  • Magnet: Separates magnetic materials (steel) from non-magnetic materials (plastic).
  • Filtration: Separates insoluble solids from liquids (e.g., orange juice pulp/seeds, or Filter B with sand & coffee filter paper).

6. Forces (Friction) & Magnets

Mid-Year Review
  • Friction: A force that slows down a moving object and acts in the opposite direction of movement.
  • Magnets: Have a North (N) and South (S) pole. Unlike poles attract; like poles repel.
  • Magnetic Materials: An object attracted to a magnet can be a magnet OR a magnetic metal (like steel/iron).

7. Living Things, Fossils & Plant Roots

Mid-Year Review
  • Fair Testing: Keep all other variables the same (leaves, water, oil layer) except the one being tested (roots).
  • Function of Roots: Roots absorb water. Plant B (without roots) will wilt and die after 10 days.
  • Fossils: Remains/imprints of ancient living things (fish, starfish, leaves) preserved in rock.
